# Resin troubleshooting and safety sources

Manufacturer documentation takes priority. Resin exposure, lift, wash, and cure settings vary by printer, resin, wavelength, temperature, and model geometry; treat all numeric adjustments as starting points requiring calibration.

## Sources

- Forge Labs SLA design guide: typical SLA tolerances, support and detail considerations.  
  https://forgelabs.com/design-guides/sla
- Protolabs Network / Hubs: hollow-part drainage, trapped uncured resin, and design guidance.  
  https://www.hubs.com/knowledge-base/how-design-parts-sla-3d-printing/
- JLC3DP: minimum escape-hole diameter and multiple escape holes for hollow resin parts.  
  https://jlc3dp.com/help/article/3d-printing-design-guideline
- Xometry: thin resin pins, fragile features, narrow slots, and small holes.  
  https://xometry.pro/en-eu/articles/3d-printing-sla-design-tips/

## Safety

Resin is hazardous before curing. Wear nitrile gloves and eye protection, ventilate the work area, avoid skin contact, and follow the resin manufacturer’s safety data sheet. Drain hollow prints before curing and do not cure sealed containers of uncured resin. Cure failed prints and disposable contaminated items before disposal where local rules require it, and never pour uncured resin down a drain.
