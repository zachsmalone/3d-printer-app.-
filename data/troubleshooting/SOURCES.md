# Troubleshooting and materials library — batch 1 sources

This batch adds the first 10 FDM troubleshooting entries. Manufacturer documentation is preferred; where a fix is a general calibration practice rather than a published manufacturer value, it is marked as requiring calibration.

## Primary sources

- Creality, “3D Printing Problems Explained: 10 Common Issues and Proven Fixes.” Used for symptom categories and general fixes including poor bed adhesion, warping, stringing, under-extrusion, layer shift, blobs, and related issues.  
  https://www.creality.com/blog/most-common-3d-printing-problems
- Prusa, “Everything about nozzles with a different diameter.” Used for the rule that layer height should not exceed about 80 percent of nozzle diameter.  
  https://blog.prusa3d.com/everything-about-nozzles-with-a-different-diameter_8344/
- Bambu Lab community discussion on nozzle size, line width, and layer height. Used only as directional guidance for the practical 75-150 percent line-width and 20-70 percent layer-height ranges.  
  https://www.reddit.com/r/BambuLab/comments/1ez3rt8/nozzle_size_and_minmax_line_width_and_layer_height/
- Bambu Lab forum discussion on extrusion width. Used to document that line width depends on nozzle size, flow, speed, and nozzle height, rather than being a fixed nozzle multiple.  
  https://forum.bambulab.com/t/why-can-a-0-4mm-nozzle-print-0-8mm/37142

## Calibration items

The following are intentionally marked **needs calibration** rather than presented as universal values:

- Retraction distance and speed
- Pressure advance / linear advance
- Flow ratio
- Z offset
- Input shaping
- Filament drying time and temperature

These depend on the exact extruder, hotend, filament brand, filament age, nozzle, and printer firmware.
