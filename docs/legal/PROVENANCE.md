# Legal drafts — provenance and status

## What these files are

Four **drafts**, written October 17, 2026, for lawyer review before launch:

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `terms-draft.md` | Terms of Service |
| `privacy-draft.md` | Privacy Policy |
| `adult-only-draft.md` | 18+ enforcement policy |
| `takedown-draft.md` | DMCA + illegal-content + community takedown process |

They are **original text written for this project**. No clause was copied from
any other provider's terms, policy, or template. Common legal concepts (DMCA
notice elements, GDPR lawful bases, OSA duty descriptions) are described in
original wording; the underlying legal facts are grounded in the official
sources listed inside each file (checked October 17, 2026). The relevant
provisions of the two statutes keying the takedown and reporting process were
extracted and used (17 U.S.C. § 512 and 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, via the Legal
Information Institute); the FTC, NCMEC, and European Commission pages were
fetched directly and their content checked before citation.

## What is deliberately missing

Every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder is a decision or fact the owner must supply
(entity name, addresses, emails, retention periods, jurisdictions). The
`[LAWYER: ...]` notes mark the points a reviewer should specifically confirm.
These drafts are **not legal advice** and must not be published as-is.

## Key grounded facts used (with sources, checked October 17, 2026)

- DMCA designated agent: online registration at dmca.copyright.gov, $6 fee per
  designation/amendment/resubmission, designation expires after 3 years unless
  renewed, same agent info must be posted publicly on the site.
  https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html
- Takedown and counter-notice elements and the 10–14 business-day restoration
  window: 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), (g)(3)–(4).
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512
- CSAM reporting duty (report to the NCMEC CyberTipline as soon as reasonably
  possible; at-least-one-year secure preservation after a completed report):
  18 U.S.C. § 2258A. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A
- EU DSA: applies to intermediary services offered in the EU since February 17,
  2024; hosting services owe notice-and-action and statements of reasons;
  micro/small enterprises are exempt from some platform-level duties, not from
  hosting duties; VLOP threshold is 45M monthly EU recipients.
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/digital-services-act-questions-and-answers
- UK Online Safety Act: illegal-content duties in force March 17, 2025;
  children's duties in force July 25, 2025; children's access assessment (s.35);
  content-reporting and complaints duties apply to regulated user-to-user
  services regardless of size.
  https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/online-safety-act and
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50
- COPPA: U.S. children's-privacy statute (FTC guidance for businesses).
  https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/childrens-privacy
- EU data protection (GDPR) overview, including third-country transfer
  safeguards. https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection_en

## Not covered by these drafts

- Entity formation, tax, trademark, or export-control advice.
- Jurisdiction-specific review outside the U.S./EU/UK notes above.
- Any court-tested position: these are drafts, not legal opinions.
