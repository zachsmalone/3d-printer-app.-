## 1. What we collect

The Service is built to work with the minimum of personal information:

- **Account basics**: an email address (for login, recovery, and safety
  notices) and a display name you choose. A date of birth is collected once at
  signup to enforce our 18+ rule; it is stored **[DECISION: store full DOB /
  store only an over-18 flag]** and is never shown publicly.
- **Files you upload**: photos and 3D model files are stored so the Service
  can process them. Private projects stay private; nothing is published unless
  you publish it.
- **Usage records**: likes, follows, print records, and moderation records,
  tied to your account.
- **Technical logs**: server logs with IP addresses for security and abuse

## 4. Safety and legal exceptions

Two things matter more than ordinary privacy rules, and this policy says
plainly how they work:

- **Child-safety reporting.** If we become aware of apparent child sexual
  abuse material, we remove it and report it — as soon as reasonably possible
  after learning of it, as U.S. law requires of providers (18 U.S.C. § 2258A:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A) — to the National
  Center for Missing and Exploited Children's CyberTipline, and to law
  enforcement where appropriate. A completed CyberTipline report requires us
to preserve the reported material securely for at least one year; that
preservation obligation overrides the deletion promises below for the reported
content. We treat this as an absolute obligation.
- **Takedown and law-enforcement requests.** We disclose account information
  to law enforcement where required by law or where we believe it is necessary
  to prevent harm. Copyright complaints are handled under our
  [Takedown Policy](takedown-draft.md).

## 6. Children

The Service is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone under 18, and we close and delete the data of
accounts we discover belong to minors, subject to the legal-preservation
exceptions in section 5. Our 18+ enforcement approach is described in the
[Adults Only policy](adult-only-draft.md).
