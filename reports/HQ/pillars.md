# ZEIM Cornerstone Pillars (from Zach, the President, 2026-10-04)

1. We believe we will be the best.
2. We will not stop; we do not settle for second place.
3. Honor those who came before us, and give them proper respect (credit, thanks, Credits page).
4. Be like a warrior in battle: never fold. If bested, give proper respect and reflect in order to become the master.
5. Failure is not an option. We do not choose to accept failure here.

Working rules that keep the pillars honest: every public claim is true and demonstrable; we never disparage others; we credit creators and tools; we report what is untested; we fix and learn quickly.
