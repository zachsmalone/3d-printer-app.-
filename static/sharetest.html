ZEIM share test (hidden page)

UNTESTED on real devices. This page exists to find out whether the iPhone/iPad share sheet accepts 3D files (STL, OBJ, 3MF) from a web page. Nobody on the team has tried it yet. It is not linked from anywhere, needs an adult account, and sends nothing to ZEIM. It shares a plain 20 mm cube made on the server. Never your photos or builds.

Tap each Share button. When the share sheet opens, note which apps are listed (Mail, Files, Shapr3D, Creality…), then cancel or pick one. Then copy the results below and send them to Haruto/Yami.
