Privacy

Draft, pending legal review. This is a plain-words summary, not a final privacy notice and not a statement of legal compliance.

ZEIM does not ask for an email address, a phone number or a real name. Accounts are for adults 18 and over. Sign-up asks for a display name and a date of birth, used only to confirm you are 18 or over. If the date shows you are under 18, no account is made and the date is not stored.

If an account is reported as belonging to someone under 18, a moderator can suspend it, and that person's data can then be deleted on request or by the server admin. If you would like something removed, press Report on it, or use Delete my stuff where it is offered.
