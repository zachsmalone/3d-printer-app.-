This page explains, in plain words, what ZEIM keeps and what you can do about it.

What ZEIM keeps

  • Your account. Accounts are for adults 18 and over. Sign-up asks for a display name and a date of birth. The date is used to check that you are 18 or over and is kept with your account. If the date shows you are under 18, no account is made and the date is not kept. ZEIM does not ask for a phone number or a real name.
  • Email (optional). You can add an email address to use one-time sign-in codes. Passkeys and passwords work without one. ZEIM never stores the address itself, only a keyed hash and a masked hint like j***@e***.com. Because of this, ZEIM cannot email you about anything except a sign-in code you asked for.
  • Sign-in. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. Passkeys store only the public key. Session cookies are stored as a hash.
  • What you make. Your uploaded photos and files, and the models and projects made from them, are stored so you can come back to them.
  • Personalization. If it is on, ZEIM keeps interest tags and recent searches for your browser to suggest projects. You can turn it off or clear it in Settings.
  • Abuse protection. Rate limits use a keyed hash of your IP address, not the address itself.
  • Server logs. The web server keeps short, rotating logs of requests, which can include IP addresses. They are used to keep the site running and safe.

Cookies and browser storage

ZEIM uses cookies only to keep you signed in, to remember your browser, and to remember that you passed the age check. Your browser also stores display settings such as theme and units. ZEIM has no ads and no third-party trackers.

Usage count: the first time this browser downloads a print file, ZEIM records one coarse number (how long since the browser first opened ZEIM, for example "5-15 minutes") and the date. It is not linked to your account, session, browser id or IP. It is not sent when Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is on, and you can turn it off under Me, Your data.

Quick question: at most once every 90 days, after a print download, ZEIM may ask how likely you are to recommend it (0 to 10). Only the number and the date are stored, not linked to you. It is never asked when usage counts are off.

How long things are kept

  • Builds are removed after 30 days. Starred builds, and projects you share to the Showcase, are kept until you delete them.
  • Your account stays until it is removed. There is no self-serve account deletion yet.

Who else handles your data

ZEIM runs on Google Cloud servers in the United States. Traffic passes through Cloudflare. Photos and models are processed on ZEIM's own server, not sent to an outside AI service. Projects you share to the Showcase can be seen by others, and content can be reviewed by moderators. ZEIM does not sell your information. Core features are free and ZEIM holds no payment details.

What you can do

  • Download my stuff (Settings) saves a file listing what ZEIM keeps about you.
  • Delete everything I made (Settings) removes all your builds, projects, photos and Showcase posts, starred ones included.
  • Delete a single build from your build list at any time.
  • Turn personalization off, or clear it, in Settings.
  • To report something, press Report on it. If an account is reported as belonging to someone under 18, a moderator can suspend it.
