Privacy

This page explains, in plain words, what ZEIM collects and what you can do about it.

Accounts are for adults 18 and over. Sign-up asks for a display name and a date of birth, used only to confirm you are 18 or over. If the date shows you are under 18, no account is made and the date is not stored. You can optionally add an email address to use one-time sign-in codes (passkeys work without an email). Emails are never stored in plain text—only a secure hash and a masked hint. ZEIM does not ask for a phone number or a real name.

If an account is reported as belonging to someone under 18, a moderator can suspend it (reversible). ZEIM does not delete accounts or their data as part of this. If you would like something removed, press Report on it, or use Delete my stuff where it is offered.

Photos you upload are used to make your 3D model. Photos and content shared on ZEIM can be reviewed by moderators. ZEIM is free for core features and takes no payments for those, so it holds no payment details. ZEIM does not sell your information. If this notice changes, we will update this page.
