If you believe something on ZEIM copies your work without permission, send a notice that includes: your name and contact details; the work you say was copied; a link to the item on ZEIM; a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised; a statement that your notice is accurate and that you own the rights or may act for the owner; and your physical or electronic signature. Send it to: nightmareopsstudio@gmail.com.

When we get a complete notice we will remove or hide the item and let the person who posted it know. If your item was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the same contact. Accounts that repeatedly post infringing work will lose access.
