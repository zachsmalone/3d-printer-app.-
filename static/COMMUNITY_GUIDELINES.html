Community rules

ZEIM accounts are for adults 18 and over. Everything on ZEIM must be clean and safe for all. Families can print designs together with an adult account holder.

Welcome

Original or properly licensed 3D-print projects; lithophanes, reliefs and pop-art made from photos you have the right to use; friendly, helpful feedback.

Please don't share

  1. Anything sexual, nude or otherwise adult.
  2. Anything that puts a child in an unsafe or sexual situation. Zero tolerance. Reports about this are hidden at once and looked at first.
  3. Graphic violence, hate, bullying or threats.
  4. Working weapons or dangerous items, or ways to hurt people.
  5. Photos of other people (especially children) without their permission, or private details such as addresses, phone numbers, school names.
  6. Work that copies someone else's copyright or trademark.
  7. Spam, scams, or anything that tries to move people to private chats.

If something looks wrong

Press Report on any project or photo and pick what fits. A report about someone's safety hides it straight away while a moderator looks at it. If you think an account belongs to someone under 18, choose The account holder seems to be under 18. Reports are read by people. Copyright complaints follow the copyright (DMCA) process in the Terms.
