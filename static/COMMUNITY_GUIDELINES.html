Community Guidelines (draft)

This product is family-friendly only, for students, teachers and hobbyists of all ages (accounts 13+; younger learners join a teacher's classroom without an account).

Allowed: original or properly licensed 3D-print projects, lithophanes, reliefs and pop-art made from photos you have the right to use; friendly, constructive feedback.

Not allowed (will be removed; repeat or severe cases lose access): 1. Anything sexual, nude, or otherwise adult. 2. Anything involving minors in a sexual, exploitative or otherwise unsafe context — zero tolerance, no exceptions. Such reports are hidden immediately and escalated. 3. Graphic violence, hate, harassment, bullying or threats. 4. Working weapons or dangerous items; instructions for harming people. 5. Photos of other people (especially children) used without their permission; personal information (addresses, phone numbers, school names). 6. Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark. 7. Spam, scams, or attempts to move young users to private channels.

Reporting: use Report on any project. Reports of type "minor safety" hide the project instantly pending review.

Draft for an MVP. Before any public launch these guidelines, the Terms of Service and the privacy policy need legal review (see ROADMAP.md → Legal & safety).
