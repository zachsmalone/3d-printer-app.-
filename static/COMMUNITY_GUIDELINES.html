Community rules

ZEIM is family-friendly, for families, kids and hobbyists of all ages. Accounts are for ages 13 and up; younger kids can use ZEIM without an account, or together with a grown-up in their family.

Welcome

Original or properly licensed 3D-print projects; lithophanes, reliefs and pop-art made from photos you have the right to use; friendly, helpful feedback.

Please don't share

  1. Anything sexual, nude or otherwise adult.
  2. Anything that puts a child in an unsafe or sexual situation. Reports about this are hidden at once and looked at first.
  3. Graphic violence, hate, bullying or threats.
  4. Working weapons or dangerous items, or ways to hurt people.
  5. Photos of other people (especially children) without their permission, or private details such as addresses, phone numbers, school names.
  6. Work that copies someone else's copyright or trademark.
  7. Spam, scams, or anything that tries to move young people to private chats.

If something looks wrong

Press Report on any project and pick what fits. A report about a person's or a child's safety hides the project straight away while a grown-up looks at it. Reports are read by people.
