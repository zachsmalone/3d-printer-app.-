# Community Guidelines (draft, pending legal review)

ZEIM accounts are for **adults 18 and over only**. All content must be **clean and safe for all**. Families can print designs together with an adult account holder.

**Allowed:** original or properly licensed 3D-print projects, lithophanes, reliefs and pop-art made from photos you have the right to use; friendly, constructive feedback.

**Not allowed (will be removed; repeat or severe cases lose access):**
1. Anything sexual, nude, or otherwise adult.
2. **Anything involving minors in a sexual, exploitative or otherwise unsafe context — zero tolerance, no exceptions.** Such reports are hidden immediately and escalated.
3. Graphic violence, hate, harassment, bullying or threats.
4. Working weapons or dangerous items; instructions for harming people.
5. Photos of other people (especially children) used without their permission; personal information (addresses, phone numbers, school names).
6. Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark.
7. Spam, scams, or attempts to move people to private channels.

**Reporting:** use *Report* on any project or print photo. Reports of type "minor safety" hide the item instantly pending review. Reports of type "underage user" (the account holder seems to be under 18) go to a moderator, who can suspend the account; its data is then deleted through the documented process (README → Underage accounts).

> Draft, pending legal review. These guidelines, the Terms and the privacy notice are not a statement of legal compliance.
